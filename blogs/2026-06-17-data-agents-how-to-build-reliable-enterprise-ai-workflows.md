Data agents are fast becoming the operating layer of enterprise AI – automating analysis, managing workflows, obtaining context, and acting across production systems. Headless agents are coming for your data, there’s no doubt about it. But while agent skills are improving at a breakneck pace, trust is still the biggest barrier to adoption.

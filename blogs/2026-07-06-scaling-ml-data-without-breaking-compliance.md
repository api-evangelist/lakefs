In highly regulated environments, improving developer experience often comes at the cost of tighter controls. For companies handling sensitive personal data, even small workflow changes can introduce compliance risks that are difficult to detect and even harder to fix at scale. The tension between usability and governance is especially visible in machine learning pipelines.

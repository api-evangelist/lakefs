A Frozen Model Is Not a Frozen System Talk to enough robotics teams and you hear the same line: “We’re using off-the-shelf models, so tracking what trained them isn’t really our problem.” It sounds reasonable. Someone else pre-trained the backbone. Your team didn’t touch the weights.
